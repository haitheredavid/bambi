# Notes

## Intent
Prototype Protoss Colossus for Archon Studio's StarCraft: Tabletop Miniatures Game (32 mm heroic scale). Multi-part kit (v4): Base, Core, Head_L/Head_R, 4 legs as mirrored halves (12 prints), joined with tabs, filament dowels and glue. PLA, 0.12 mm.

Scale rationale: official TMG bases are 32 (Marine), 40 (Zealot), 50 (Sentry), 80 (Stalker), 100 (Immortal), 150 mm (Siege Tank), all with 5x3 mm magnet slots. The lore Colossus is ~52 m, which would be ~930 mm at true scale, so this uses game scale instead: ~2.3x a Stalker, so ~170 mm tall on a 120 mm round base with a sloped rim, terrain on top and a magnet recess underneath.

## Iterations
- v1 (2026-09-26): 6 parts on one plate: Base (Ø120 x 4 mm, sloped rim 60 to 57 mm, 0.5 mm bottom chamfer, rocks and plating up to 5.8 mm, Ø5.3 x 3.2 mm magnet recess, 4 rectangular foot sockets 5.6 x 6.6 x 2.7 mm on the diagonals at r=50), Hull (68 x 61 x 43 mm, flat belly, hex lance emitters on 45° arms, twin swept fins), 4 identical legs (166 x 39 x 6 mm, printed flat). Assembled height about 171 mm (hull belly at z=128, fin tips at 171). Slice: 2h 32m, 64.6 g, 0.12 mm, PLA, Textured PEI, no supports. Changes from the plan: round pegs became full-thickness rectangular tabs (hip tab 4.5 x 4, foot tab 5 x 2.5), because round pegs would float off the bed when the legs print flat; this also keys the rotation. Blender: the `Assembly` collection (assembled preview, source `Leg` mesh) is excluded from the view layer, and `Plate` holds the exported, print-oriented instances that share its meshes. ## Blender layout (model.blend)
- `Scene`: the active scene for export. `Plate` = print-oriented parts (only these export); `Assembly` = assembled preview (`*_asm`, excluded from the view layer); `Work2` = builder scratch (excluded).
- `Assembly Anim`: the assembly animation (`just anim colossus-tmg`). Uses the same meshes as `Assembly`, so it picks up rebuilds.
- `Render`: studio scene for material previews. Links the `*_asm` objects, 3 area lights (Key/Rim/Fill), a floor, and `RenderCam`.
- Save with `Scene` active before `just build`: export reads the saved active scene.

## Rendering
- Live preview: switch the scene dropdown (top right) to `Render`, set viewport shading to Rendered (Z → Rendered), then press Numpad 0 for the camera view and F12 to render a still.
- Palette (builders/render.py `PAL`): M_Armor gold metal, M_Body dark khaydarin purple, M_Metal gunmetal, M_Glow blue emission, M_Base dark stone. EEVEE, AgX Medium High Contrast, exposure -0.8.
- Materials come from tags set on each primitive in the builders. Booleans use `material_mode='TRANSFER'`, so cut grooves take the cutter's material (glow lines). They only affect renders; the STL is unchanged.
- Stills: `out/renders/{hero,front,side,back,head}.png` (1200x1500) and the strip `out/2026-09-26-colossus-tmg-renders.png`. To redo them, run `g['r_shoot'](sc, path, az, el, dist, target, lens)` over MCP (front = +x, az in degrees).

## Rebuilding
- `exec(open('<session>/builders/build_all.py').read(), {'__name__': 'build_all'})` over MCP. It runs lib.py (helpers into driver_namespace['cz2']), head.py, core_leg.py, install.py (interference check, split halves, plate update) and render.py, and refreshes the anim meshes. It survives Blender restarts.
- Key dims live at the top of head.py (`Z0`, `PROF`, `DISCS`, cowl `C0/C1/CW`) and core_leg.py (`HIP`, `KNEE`, `FOOT`, `NECK_TOP`). FOOT and the foot tab must stay fixed unless the base sockets change.
- Lessons: EXACT booleans go non-manifold on coincident end caps and coplanar faces (rod tips, nozzle collars, overlapping fins), so offset them by 0.3-0.5 mm. Ray-parity interference hits that sit exactly on the z=103 neck/head face are contact, not overlap.

## Assembly
- Dowels: 1.75 mm filament in Ø2.1 holes. 30 in total: 6 per leg (24, glue the halves), 4 for the head halves, 2 from the neck into the head (v3 had 26).
- Order: base (magnet in) → Core → legs (hip tab into the hub socket, foot tab into the base socket) → glued head onto the neck.
- Core prints upside down on the neck's top face; head halves and leg halves print cut-face down.

## Open risks / next ideas
- The dowel and tab fit is untested. The head (~2x30 g at normal infill) sits on the Ø11-13 neck, so glue it well.
- The fin stem is ~3.5 mm and fragile. Lance rods are Ø2.6-3.2 on ball joints.
- Not modelled: circuit engraving on the armour (below printable depth, so paint it) and smooth curved leg blades like the sculpt.
