# Notes

## Intent
6" (150 mm) putty knife for spreading joint compound on medium apartment wall patches. Printed flat in PLA Basic: blade tapers 3 mm -> 1 mm edge, 100 mm handle, cat head with ears at the butt and blue silk eyes inlaid flush in the top face (2-colour AMS). Body orange PLA Basic (slot 3), eyes blue PLA Silk (slot 0).

## Iterations
- v1 (2026-09-30): `Knife` 236.5 x 150 x 16 mm (convex-hull parts unioned: blade 150->40 wide, 75 long, 3->1 mm top-side taper, R3 edge corners; neck ramp; 100 mm handle 28x16 w/ R5 top; Ø44 head w/ ears at ±40°, 14 thick; 0.4 bottom chamfer except blade; engraved nose 0.6 deep). `Eyes` 2x 6x9 mm ellipses, 1 mm inlay flush with head top. Floating eyes failed slicing as a separate object ("empty layer between 0 and 15.2"), so added `[slice] assemble = true` (assemble_index merges STLs into one object). Slice: 1h 15m, 47.5 g (46.3 white + 1.2 blue, 5 colour changes w/ prime tower), 0.20mm_standard, Textured PEI. Compromises: blade edge 1 mm PLA, no bottom chamfer on blade; slot 2 must hold white PLA Basic (currently Support for PLA).
- v1b (2026-09-30): body switched to orange PLA Basic (slot 3); slot 2 white is Bambu Support for PLA (GFS02 via RFID), unsuitable as body and PLA eyes would not bond to it.
- v2 (2026-09-30): v1 snapped immediately at the blade/handle joint: the 150 mm blade funnelled all load through a 40 x 3 mm heel with a crease into the 3->16 mm neck (weak section + stress raiser, brittle PLA). Research (steel knives: full tang + bolster; moulded plastic knives: reinforced rib fading into the blade; snap-fit guides: taper so strain spreads, root fillet >= 0.5 t) -> rebuilt parametrically in `builders/knife.py --variant spine`: lofted sections, bell-shaped planform 150 -> 28 mm flowing tangentially into the handle at x=115 (no throat), plate 1.2 mm edge -> 3 mm at x=55, a tang-like spine (cos² bump -> rounded-box handle) rising from x=20 to full 16 mm at x=125. Section modulus per unit moment roughly constant along the blade (~4-5x lower stress than v1 before stress concentration). Slice: `[slice.overrides]` 5 walls, 40% gyroid, 0.16mm_optimal -> 3h 00m, 79.0 g (77.6 orange + 1.4 blue). v1 geometry kept in `model-v1.blend`. Sibling design: `2026-09-30-cat-putty-knife-slope`.
- v2b (2026-10-01): single colour on request: blue Eyes inlay dropped (`knife.py --variant spine --no-eyes`), eye pockets kept as 1 mm engravings; one filament, orange PLA Basic slot 3, no assemble. Slice: 2h 41m, 73.9 g.
- v2c (2026-10-01): round Ø44 head removed; the handle section runs to a butt end at x=230 with R6 corners, and triangular ears grow past the end, trimmed (boolean INTERSECT) to the handle cross-section so the cat head stays 28 x 16 like the grip. Eyes (2.2 x 3.2 ellipses, floor z=14.2) and the 0.6 mm nose are engraved into the crown. 241.8 x 149.8 x 16 mm. Slice: 2h 21m, 66.9 g.
