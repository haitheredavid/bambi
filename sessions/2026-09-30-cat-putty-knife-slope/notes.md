# Notes

## Intent
One-piece cat putty knife in the pizza-server style: the top rises steadily from a 1.2 mm working edge all the way into the 28 x 16 mm handle, no neck step. Alternative to `2026-09-30-cat-putty-knife` v2 (spine), after v1 snapped at the blade/handle joint. Orange PLA Basic body (slot 3), blue PLA Silk eyes inlaid (slot 0).

## Iterations
- v1 (2026-09-30): built by `../2026-09-30-cat-putty-knife/builders/knife.py --variant slope` (shared builder). Planform tapers concavely 150 -> 28 mm over 130 mm (pizza slice, tangent into the handle); thickness crown across the full width, centreline near-linear ramp 1.2 -> 16 mm over 130 mm easing into the handle top; edges 1.2 -> 3 mm. Same cat head, ears, eyes and nose as the spine variant. 242.5 x 148.5 x 16 mm, manifold. Slice: 5 walls, 40% gyroid, 0.16mm_optimal -> 3h 21m, 83.0 g (81.6 orange + 1.4 blue). Stiffer than the spine version close to the handle, less flex in the blade.
