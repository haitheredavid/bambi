# Notes

## Intent
Five world door styles on a shared 186 x 30 x 3 mm base, 36 mm pitch, 30 x 8 mm frames stepping 50 to 68 mm tall: Japanese shoji, Dutch stable door, Moorish horseshoe arch, Romanesque round arch, Gothic pointed arch. Prints upright with no supports, PLA, 0.16 mm layers.

## Iterations
- v1 (2026-09-26): all five doors fused with the base into one manifold `DoorSampler` (186 x 30 x 68 mm, 1 shell). Slice: 1h 56m, 48.1 g, 0.16 mm, PLA, Textured PEI. Reliefs 0.5-0.8 mm deep; the finest parts are the Moorish voussoir grooves (0.5 mm) and the Gothic trefoil (1.3 mm lobes), which may come out soft. Pinnacles are 2 mm square: fragile. Note: `just new` did not switch an already-open Blender to the new file (it was still on calib-cube), so I opened it through MCP.
